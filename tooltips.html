How contextual help works

Each help button explains one decision. You can reach every button using Tab, keep the explanation open with a tap, and close it with Escape.

Common questions

Can I change these settings later?

Yes. Adjust a setting and save again. Restore defaults returns the form and summary to the original configuration.

What is the difference between notifications and a digest?

Notifications cover individual mentions and assignments. A digest combines activity into one weekly summary.

Who should use a review step?

Teams that need a second check before completing work, such as editorial, design, or quality assurance teams.
